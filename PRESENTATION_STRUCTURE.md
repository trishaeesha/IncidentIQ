# IncidentIQ Final Presentation Structure

## Slide 1 — Title

IncidentIQ: Human-AI Decision Support for Evidence-Grounded Software Incident
Troubleshooting

## Slide 2 — Problem

Software incidents contain:

- large telemetry volumes
- conflicting evidence
- incomplete information
- misleading signals
- uncertainty

## Slide 3 — Research Question

Under what software-incident conditions does evidence-grounded AI assistance
improve or degrade human engineers' diagnostic decision-making?

## Slide 4 — Proposed Solution

IncidentIQ provides evidence-grounded decision support while keeping the human
engineer responsible for the final diagnosis.

## Slide 5 — Research Design

Three modes:

- Human-only
- Generic AI
- IncidentIQ

Five evidence conditions:

- Clear
- Ambiguous
- Conflicting
- Incomplete
- Misleading

## Slide 6 — Incident Cases

Show the five study cases and their evidence characteristics.

## Slide 7 — System Architecture

Telemetry/evidence
→ diagnosis
→ uncertainty
→ AI assistance
→ human decision
→ outcome
