## Limitations

- Participant data is observational.
- Exploratory ML does not establish causality.
- Participant identity must not be used as a predictive feature.
- RCAEval cases are not sufficient for predictive ML claims.
- NLP/NLI behavior must be based on actual execution.
- No autonomous remediation is claimed.
